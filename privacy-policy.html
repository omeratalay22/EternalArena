English

1. Introduction

Eternal Arena (the “App”, “we”, or “our”) is a mobile arena action game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App and the choices available to you.

2. Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account, login, or registration, and does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

Game progress, settings, language preference, audio settings, and similar gameplay information are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this local gameplay data. You can remove it by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's behavior.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google, to display banner advertisements. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and privacy settings, Google may process information such as your IP address, advertising or device identifiers, device model, operating system and App information, approximate location, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, limit, and protect advertising.

For more information, review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob policies

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record your advertising consent choices before requesting ads. You may refuse consent or manage the available options in the consent message. You can also manage advertising privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings where available.

5. Data sharing and retention

We do not directly sell personal information or maintain a user database. Google may process, share, and retain advertising-related information as an independent third-party service provider according to its own policies, your consent choices, and applicable law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through direct contact, please email us so that we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures within the App to protect locally stored gameplay information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party services apply their own security practices.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. Because we do not maintain a user account or server-side user database, we may not be able to identify data held by third parties. Requests about Google's processing should also be directed to Google using the controls and contact methods in Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: atalay3002@gmail.com
